Welcome to cabellite guide

A browser-based TV interface where the guide is the system. Live channels, DVR, multiview, on-demand, apps, weather, media, sports, games, trivia, adventure, coloring, crossword, kids multiview, and trending — all from the guide.

Getting started
Dismissing the screensaver
Press Space to dismiss the screensaver. The guide opens on your last channel.
The Guide
Press G to open or close the guide from anywhere except Search. Hover a row to preview it. Click the video preview to go fullscreen. Press Esc or G to exit fullscreen.

Interactive channels
Trivia TV (702)
3 rounds × 10 questions from a 1,000-question bank. 30-second think timer, answer locks in until the timer ends, gong then ding. 1–4 / A–D to answer.
Choose Your Adventure (705)
Branching stories with 100+ story starts and ~1,000 endings. Pick a story, make choices, reach an ending, read again. 1–3 to choose.
Coloring TV (79)
Pick a page, pick a color, click a region to fill it. Undo, clear, save as PNG.
Crossword TV (1234)
1,000 generated crosswords. Click a cell, type or use the on-screen keyboard. Hints, reveal, check.
Kids Multiview (195)
Like Multiview (190), but only kid-friendly M3U channels are selectable. Pick 4, click a small pane to swap.
DVR
Recording a channel
Select a channel in the guide and press REC in the preview bar. Recordings are saved in IndexedDB.
Scheduling a recording
Press Schedule in the preview bar. Scheduled recordings run while cabellite guide is open.
Multiview
Watching 4 channels at once
Go to channel 190. Pick up to 4 channels. The big pane has audio; the small panes are muted. Click a small pane to swap.
On Demand
Loading an On-Demand playlist
Open channel 980 (Packages) and upload an On Demand M3U. Titles appear in the On Demand menu.
Games
Games hub
Any uploaded HTML app with a game-like name is automatically collected in the Games hub at channel 3500, grouped by detected genre.
Data
What persists
Playlists, favorites, DVR recordings, multiview lineup, kids multiview lineup, uploaded HTML apps, and keyboard position are saved in your browser. Media uploads (channel 1000) do not persist.
