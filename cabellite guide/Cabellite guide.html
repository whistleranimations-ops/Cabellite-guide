Welcome to cabellite guide

A browser-based TV interface where the guide is the system. Live channels, DVR, multiview, on-demand, apps, weather, media, sports, and trending — all from the guide.

Getting started
Dismissing the screensaver
Press Space to dismiss the screensaver. The guide opens on your last channel.
The Guide
Press G to open or close the guide from anywhere except Search. Hover a row to preview it. Click the video preview to go fullscreen. Press Esc or G to exit fullscreen.

DVR
Recording a channel
Select a channel in the guide and press REC in the preview bar. Recordings are saved in IndexedDB.
Scheduling a recording
Press Schedule in the preview bar. Scheduled recordings run while cabellite guide is open.
Multiview
Watching 4 channels at once
Go to channel 190. Pick up to 4 channels. The big pane has audio; the small panes are muted. Click a small pane to swap.
On Demand
Loading an On-Demand playlist
Open channel 980 (Packages) and upload an On Demand M3U. Titles appear in the On Demand menu.
Data
What persists
Playlists, favorites, DVR recordings, multiview lineup, uploaded HTML apps, and keyboard position are saved in your browser. Media uploads (channel 1000) do not persist.
